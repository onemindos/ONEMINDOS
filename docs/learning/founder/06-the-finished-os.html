This is OneMind OS as it looks once every epic in the backlog has landed. Every machine you own, from a Raspberry Pi in the garage to a GPU box and a cloud server, joins one system. Your apps, files, devices and AI agents live on it. AI can see all of it when you allow it, and nothing physical or risky happens until you approve it on your phone.

The One Mind fabric

One OneMind, many machines

A site is one place: your home, the greenhouse, an office. Each site runs on its own and keeps working offline. Sites connect to each other through a fleet hub. Pick any box in the picture.

Decision D-002 · one design, every tier

Every machine has a superpower

The same image pipeline builds every tier. A tier is a profile, not a fork. Apps say what they need, and the OS decides which machine can run them.
