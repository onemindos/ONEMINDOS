# DC-F1 refuter, longevity / vendor-risk lens

**Claim given:** as DC-F1. **Cited:** C-W4-056 (CNCF statement), C-FB3-002.

**Strongest counter-argument.** NATS's main corporate maintainer tried in 2025 to take the project out of CNCF and move future releases from Apache-2.0 to the Business Source License [C-FBREF-007]. A bus that a single company can relicense is a ten-year risk for an Apache-2.0 OS.

**New sources:** C-FBREF-007 (The Register, 2025-05-02, SECONDARY), C-FB11-001 (release cadence: v2.15.3 on 2026-11-11, i.e. active development after the dispute).

**Verdict: upheld.** The dispute ended with the trademarks assigned to the Linux Foundation and the code and repositories staying under Apache-2.0 at CNCF [C-FBREF-007; C-W4-056]; releases continue. A fork of an Apache-2.0 Go server is feasible if ever needed.

**What would flip it:** a nats-server release under a non-OSI licence, or CNCF archiving the project. Watch item for the licence matrix re-check (12 months).
