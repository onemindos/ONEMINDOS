# Competitors: industrial edge (FB-11, group 2)

**Date:** 2026-11-12. Patterns only.

> **Explain it like I'm the founder.** Industrial edge platforms sell a central "manager" (on-prem or cloud) that onboards approved edge boxes, pushes containerised apps to them, and connects to factory machines through drivers (OPC UA, Modbus, PLC protocols). The closest open-source one, Red Hat's Flight Control, already does owner-approved enrollment and rollouts in batches, which is exactly what OneMind designed. The cloud ones (Azure, AWS) make the cloud the boss and limit how long a site may stay offline; OneMind does not.
