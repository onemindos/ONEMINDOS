# Competitors: fleet, robotics and physical AI (FB-11, group 1)

**Date:** 2026-11-12 · Deepens mission 01's `02-competitors/robotics-*.md` on the **join and fleet model** only. Patterns only from non-Apache-compatible code.

> **Explain it like I'm the founder.** The robotics platforms all do the same three things: a small agent on the robot that calls out to a cloud, a way to see and drive the robot remotely, and a way to push software to many robots. None of them lets the robot's owner run everything without the vendor's cloud, and none puts a human approval in front of an AI moving hardware. That is OneMind's opening.
