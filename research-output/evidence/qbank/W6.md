## Corrections found

- Q63 (owner W7) says the EU Machinery Regulation applies from 2027-01-20. The official text (Art. 54) says **14 January 2027** [C-W6-043].
- ADR-001 D4 says RKE2 has "FIPS 140-2 builds". The certificate RKE2's docs cite is Historical since September 2026, and CJIS v6.1 rejects 140-2 certificates after 2026-09-21 [C-W6-025, -026, -033].
