It is currently not possible to submit proposals, but there is no rush: the next deadline is nearly two months away. You can of course already check out the questions.
Through this form you can request financial support for your project. NLnet runs several funds with different themes and eligibility requirements, so
learn more about our funding
and see if any fund matches. Some general requirements:
Our grants are primarily for
technical development
.
Software, hardware and content must be released under
open licences
and apply (preferably open)
standards
.
Proposals and projects should be made
by humans, not AI
. See our
Generative AI policy
.
Our EU-supported funds require a strong
European dimension
; usually this means applicants should (mostly) be from Horizon Europe-eligible countries.
