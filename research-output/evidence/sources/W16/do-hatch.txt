Frequently asked questions
Are applications for the DigitalOcean Startups program currently open?
Applications for our current cohort are temporarily paused as we prepare to launch an updated program experience in early 2027.
When will the new DigitalOcean Startups program launch?
The new program is scheduled to launch in early 2027.
I am a current Hatch / Startups program participant. How does this affect my benefits?
Active program members are not affected by this pause. Your existing DigitalOcean credits, 1-on-1 technical advisory sessions, and partner perks remain fully active through the duration of your original program term. You can continue accessing your benefits via your founder dashboard or by contacting [email-masked].
Are you still accepting new accelerator, VC, and incubator partners?
Yes! We actively welcome new venture partners and portfolio managers. If you manage an accelerator, VC fund, or startup community, please reach out via [email-masked] to discuss co-marketing opportunities and early partner access for 2027.
What is DigitalOcean Startups?
DigitalOcean Startups helps developers create, launch, and scale their startups. This includes a range of perks to get you started, including 12 months of DigitalOcean credits to easily deploy and scale your infrastructure.
Why is DigitalOcean doing this?
We want to empower the startup community. We always strive to support and show love to our community. We want it to be easy for you to have access to our resources.
Who is eligible for DigitalOcean Startups? How do you select DigitalOcean Startups members?
We prioritize AI-native startups. Service-based startups (consulting services, web/app development services, marketing agencies, etc.) are not eligible. New applicants are evaluated using the following criteria:
Must have raised $10m or less.
Must have a company website, and an email address associated with the company website.
Must create a registered DigitalOcean team account with a business/company email (not a personal email account).
How do I know if my accelerator/incubator/VC firm has a relationship with the DigitalOcean Startups?
Please see our
portfolio partner list
. You can ask your program manager if you have a partnership with DigitalOcean Startups.
How many credits do I get?
DigitalOcean awards startups credits through approved DigitalOcean Startups partners. To learn more about your eligibility award level, please contact your program manager at your DigitalOcean Startups Partner.
What if I have already received credits for my startup?
This program is for new startups that have not used prior credits with DigitalOcean.
How do DigitalOcean credits work?
You will be given a pre-determined amount of credits as set out in your onboarding email to be used over the term of the program with Qualifying Program Services. Participants generally are entitled to a $10,000 monthly limit (and charges apply for any use in a month exceeding this amount).
When accepted into the program, what types of services can I use with my credits ("Qualifying Program Services")?
As of May 13, 2026, you may use your credits on any DigitalOcean services, except as to the following:
GPU Droplets
NVIDIA H100 GPU Kubernetes
Bare Metal GPUs
Dedicated Inference
Serverless Inference, Third-party AI models hosted outside of DigitalOcean Infrastructure (including commercial/frontier models, including but not limited to Anthropic and OpenAI)
Paperspace Machines
Cloudways services
Any other charges passed through to third-party providers outside of DigitalOcean's infrastructure
This set of credits are referred to as “core” credits. GPU credits are a separate benefit and will not be covered by the core credits received when initially accepted into the program. Acceptance into the program does not ensure entitlement of GPU credit benefits, and are made available to select customers after review of their GPU credit application and infrastructure needs.
Not sure if a certain product is covered by your credits? Reach out to
[email-masked]
and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
Do DigitalOcean credits cover GPU Droplet usage?
DigitalOcean Startups core credits do not cover your GPU Droplet usage, and may only be used with Qualifying Program Services. Your account's payment method will be charged for usage of GPU Droplets and any other services not appearing on the Qualifying Program Services.
Can you help migrate my customers from other public clouds to DigitalOcean?
Yes, we have migration plans, technical assistance, and credits available to make it easy for your clients.
How do I receive access to the DigitalOcean Startups perks?
You can access DigitalOcean Startups' perks through the Welcome Kit that you receive when you're accepted into the program.
What happens if I go over the monthly credit limit?
If you go over the monthly credit limit, you will be charged for the additional usage for that month.
Do I need to be a DigitalOcean customer to sign up for DigitalOcean Startups?
Before applying for DigitalOcean Startups, you must
create a new DigitalOcean team account
for your startup with 1) the corporate email address associated with your company domain and 2) a valid
credit card. You can learn more about DigitalOcean team accounts
here
. For security reasons, applicants must include a working website and matching corporate email address.
Can I transfer my DigitalOcean credits?
No. The DigitalOcean credits are designed to help you scale your infrastructure and are not transferable. If you do not use the credits, they will be forfeited.
Are there any other limitations on how I use DigitalOcean credits?
Yes. Program credits may only be used for Qualifying Program Services, which excludes GPU Products. Additionally, you cannot use any program credits for the mining of cryptocurrency (e.g. bitcoin mining). You can also not use the DigitalOcean credits for services in a manner that consumes excessive network capacity, CPU cycles, or disk IO, or that are AI model inference services hosted by third party vendors. If you do, use of services other than Qualifying Program Services are subject to additional charges, and DigitalOcean may end your participation in the DigitalOcean Startups program. Please review the
DigitalOcean Startups Terms of Service
for complete program terms.
I haven't been able to spend all my credits. Can I get an extension?
The DigitalOcean Startups program does not grant extensions.
