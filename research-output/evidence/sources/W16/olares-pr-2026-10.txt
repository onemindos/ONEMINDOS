Olares Showcases Private AI at Intel's AI NAS & Thunderbolt™ Technology Forum in Japan
News provided by
OLARES TECH CO., LTD.
Oct 15, 2026, 09:00 ET
Share this article
Share this article
The open-source platform brings agentic AI to hardware people own, giving users greater control over how their AI runs and how their data is used.
TOKYO
,
Oct. 15, 2026
/PRNewswire/ -- Olares, the company behind the open-source
Olares OS
, today showcased its operating system on Intel® Core™ Ultra–powered AI NAS at Intel's AI NAS & Thunderbolt™ Technology Forum in Tokyo. The showcase highlighted how users can run AI agents that work with their files and applications on hardware they own.
Continue Reading
Olares Showcases Private AI at Intel’s AI NAS & Thunderbolt™ Technology Forum in Japan
Running a model is only one part of building a useful personal AI system. Agents also need access to the files, applications and tools involved in a task. Olares OS brings these resources together in one environment, helping users put AI agents to work in their everyday workflows.
"Choosing a personal AI system is a long-term decision," said Peng Peng, Founder and CTO of Olares. "People should be able to change the models they use, add applications and decide where their data is processed as their needs evolve. That flexibility is central to how we're building Olares OS."
Agents, models and apps in one personal cloud
Key capabilities of Olares OS include:
Shared access to AI models.
Olares Router gives agents and applications a common gateway to local models and other configured AI services, with model connections and access controls managed in one place.
Task execution in plain language.
Lares, the Olares-native AI agent, uses configured models and tools to carry out multi-step tasks across files, applications and system services. Users can ask Lares to handle everyday tasks such as organizing files and checking system status. It can also take on more complex work, including deploying applications, conducting in-depth research, and generating images and videos through FlowStudio, an AI media creation app.
An ecosystem of more than 200 apps.
Olares Market
offers one-click installation of apps and supported AI models. Users can deploy models such as Qwen3.8-27B alongside apps including Home Assistant for smart home integration and n8n for workflow automation.
AI across work, creativity and home.
From transcribing meeting recordings to generating images, videos and music locally, Olares supports a range of personal AI workflows. With the relevant integrations configured, users can also ask Lares to control connected smart home devices in natural language.
These capabilities are accessible across devices, at home or on the go, while the underlying apps, local models and data remain on users' own hardware.
Olares One
, the company's personal AI cloud device, pairs an Intel Core Ultra 9 275HX processor with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090 Mobile GPU to support demanding local generative AI workloads. Olares OS can also be installed on other compatible systems, with supported workloads depending on the hardware configuration.
"Home is the new edge," said Gordon Sylin, Global Head of NAS at Intel. "The AI NAS is growing from the place you store your life into the place your agents live, and Intel Core Ultra was built for exactly this: edge AI that runs all day, at home, on hardware people own. Olares OS shows what our partner ecosystem can deliver when the operating system is designed for agents from day one."
An open platform for partners
Olares is inviting AI NAS manufacturers, original design manufacturers (ODMs) and system integrators to explore Olares OS for compatible Intel Core Ultra–based systems. Its open-source software and application ecosystem provide a foundation for products that combine personal storage with AI agents and applications.
Olares One raised $2.34 million on Kickstarter in January 2026 and has fulfilled all backer orders. To learn more about Olares OS and explore its open-source code, visit
olares.com
and
github.com/beclab/Olares
.
Availability
Olares One is available at
shop.olares.com
, with Black Friday offers planned for November.
About Olares
Founded in 2022 and headquartered in Singapore, Olares develops personal AI cloud software and devices. Its open-source operating system, Olares OS, enables individuals and teams to run AI models, agents and applications on their own hardware, with control over their data. Olares One is the company's personal AI cloud device, built to run Olares OS. Olares has raised more than $100 million, including a Series A led by SIG. Learn more at
olares.com
and
github.com/beclab/Olares
.
