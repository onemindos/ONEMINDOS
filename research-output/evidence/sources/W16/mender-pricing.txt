Pricing FAQ
How does pricing work for Mender Enterprise?
Pricing for Mender Enterprise is tailored to each customer's needs and depends on each customer's specific requirements, number of devices, SLA parameters, and more.
How do the plans differ between hosted and on-premise for Mender Enterprise?
The
on-premise
Enterprise plan allows you to run Mender anywhere: a
private cloud
(your own datacenter and Kubernetes), or a
public cloud provider
(Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud Platform, Microsoft Azure), or
on top of Kubernetes engines
(Amazon EKS, Google Kubernetes Engine).
The
hosted
Enterprise plan allows you to use the secure Mender server we host for you, avoiding the need for the expertise or associated costs of running a server and IT infrastructure.
Can you increase the device limit of a specific plan?
Current hosted Mender customers paying by credit card can add devices to their existing accounts.
Adding devices is fully self-service. Set your new device limit in the
subscription portal
, and the change takes effect immediately.
If you require a device limit beyond 2000, such as 3000 devices on the Basic plan, simply
contact us
, and we'll create a pricing proposal for you.
Can I evaluate Mender for free?
Yes.
Sign up for hosted Mender
and you can evaluate all the technical features Mender Enterprise offers.* The trial is free for 12 months, and there is no credit card required to sign up.
Follow our getting started guide with a Raspberry Pi board, ESP32, or virtual device, and you can be up and running in minutes.
(*Mender Enterprise free trial excludes Service Provider functionality and SLA-backed support.
Free trial users receive
best effort support
.)
