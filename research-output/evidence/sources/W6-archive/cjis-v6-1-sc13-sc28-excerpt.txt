SC-13 CRYPTOGRAPHIC PROTECTION 
[Existing] [Priority 2]  
Control:  
a. Determine the use of encryption for CJI in-transit when outside a physically secure location; 
and  
b. Implement the following types of cryptography required for each specified cryptographic 
use: cryptographic modules which are Federal Information Processing Standard (FIPS) 140-3 
certified, or a FIPS validated algorithm for symmetric key encryption and decryption (FIPS 
197 [AES]), with a symmetric cipher key of at least 256-bit strength for CJI in-transit.  
NOTE: Subsequent versions of approved cryptographic modules that are under current review for 
FIPS 140-3 compliancy can be used in the interim until certification is complete.  FIPS 140 -2 
certificates will not be acceptable after September 21, 2026.

SC-28 PROTECTION OF INFORMATION AT REST 
[Existing] [Priority 2]  
Control:  
Protect the confidentiality and integrity of the following information at rest: CJI when outside 
physically secure locations  using cryptographic modules which are certified FIPS 140-3 with a 
symmetric cipher key of at least 256-bit strength, or FIPS 197 with a symmetric cipher key of at 
least 256-bit strength.
