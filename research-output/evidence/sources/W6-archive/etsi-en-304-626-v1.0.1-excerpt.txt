5.2.5 TR-SCUD: Secure updates 
5.2.5.1 MI-KEVD: Documentation for secure update before or during first use 
Assessment criteria: [AC-KEVD] 
The product shall be accompanied by documentation describing how the product may be securely updated, including 
how to update the product prior to, or as part of, first use. 
• Applicability: The product has firmware or software update capability 
5.2.5.2 MI-KEVA: Secure update before or during first use 
Assessment criteria: [AC-KEVA] 
The product shall implement a secure update mechanism that operates by default, including before or during first use.  
The product shall provide a clear and easy-to-use mechanism allowing an authorised user to opt out of automatic 
security updates. 
If an authorised user opted out of automatic security updates, the product shall provide, before or during first use, a 
mechanism to inform the user to initiate or postpone the update. 
• Applicability: The product has firmware or software update capability

5.2.5.3 MI-SCUE: Security updates provided by the operational environment 
Assessment criteria: [AC-SCUE] 
The product shall be capable of receiving and applying secure updates provided by the operational environment, with 
their authenticity and integrity verified in accordance with MI-SUVI. The manufacturer shall document the 
requirements that the operational environment shall satisfy to deliver secure updates.  
5.2.5.4 MI-SUVI: Update authenticity and integrity verification 
Assessment criteria: [AC-SUVI] 
The product shall verify the authenticity and integrity of update packages before installation. For a given update path, 
this verification may be satisfied by any one of the following mechanisms: 
• verification of a cryptographic digital signature on the update package by the product;  
• verification of update integrity only, by a hash or checksum, where authenticity is assured by compensating 
controls such as restricted access control, an authenticated delivery channel, or controlled tooling;  
• an authenticated secure channel between the product and an authorised update source; 
• controlled local or offline update through authorised personnel, trusted tools, and controlled update media; or 
• a trusted platform component, such as a package manager or installer, that verifies the authenticity and 
integrity of update packages and controls their installation. 
Where cryptographic mechanisms are used, they shall conform to TR-CRYP. 
NOTE: For image-based operating systems, installation means activating the new image. 
• Applicability: The product has firmware or software update capability 
5.2.5.5 MI-SUDP: Anti-rollback protection 
Assessment criteria: [AC-SUDP] 
The product shall maintain a monotonic version counter or an equivalent mechanism to prevent the unauthor ised 
installation of an earlier software version. 
• Applicability: The product has firmware or software update capability 
5.2.5.6 MI-SUAR: Authorised rollback 
Assessment criteria: [AC-SUAR] 
Where the product supports intentional, user-initiated rollback to an earlier software version, the rollback shall require 
explicit authorisation. Automated recovery mechanisms that revert to the immediately preceding operational state 
following a failed update or boot failure are considered recovery actions and are exempt from this explicit author isation 
requirement. 
• Applicability: The product supports intentional, user-initiated rollback to an earlier software version

5.2.7 TR-CDST: Confidentiality of data stored on the product 
5.2.7.1 MI-ENST: Cryptographic encryption of stored data 
Assessment criteria: [AC-ENST] 
Where the product stores user data, the product shall, by default, provide the option to protect the confidentiality of that 
data at rest using a cryptographic encryption mechanism conforming to TR -CRYP. 
• Applicability: Product stores user data
