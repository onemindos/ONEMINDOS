Benefits 

Joining the Works with Home Assistant program allows you to
								showcase the official badge of compatibility, which is also
								displayed on your integration page.

Only certified partners are permitted to use the dedicated
								'Works with' badge.

Your devices will be promoted through Home Assistant’s official
								channels. Most importantly, these devices will become the
								preferred choice for over two million households using Home
								Assistant.

What's involved? 

Step 1  of 6  
 Choose the products you would like to certify  
We can advise on our testing availability and help you prioritize your device list.   

Step 2  of 6  
 Sign our WWHA agreement  
This lays out clearly each party’s responsibilities to each other.   

Step 3  of 6  
 Pay the annual fee payment of 500 Swiss Francs  
Designed to cover admin only, so the program can still be a great fit for start-up companies.   

Step 4  of 6  
 Our expert team tests devices  
Let us know if you require any customizations to help our team start using your devices.   

Step 5  of 6  
 Testing feedback is given  
Not simply a pass/fail: Feedback rounds are imperative to help us address issues and keep user experience a top priority.   

Step 6  of 6  
 Approval and WWHA badge awarded  
Use the badge on product listings, web pages, packaging, and other marketing material to showcase your connection with our community.     

Program goals for 2026 

What's required? 

End Devices 

We do not currently certify connecting devices such as hubs, bridges, repeaters or Zigbee / Z-Wave adapters.   

Available to the general public 

If your device is going through a crowd-funding or pre-sale campaign, we will not test it until it is fully funded and in the manufacturing stage.   

Available locally and not reliant on a separate cloud connection 

We prioritize local control above everything else. Cloud control can be an extra, but must be opt-in only.   

Available to access firmware updates directly in Home Assistant 

Ideally, all devices should be able to access over-the-air (OTA) firmware updates from within our system. If this isn't possible, you must provide a way to alert users about firmware updates.   

Zigbee 

CSA Certification  

Matter 

CSA Certification  

Z-Wave 

Z-Wave Alliance Certification  

Local network or Bluetooth connectivity 

An integration quality of gold or above    

All devices must have FCC/CE or equivalent certification 

Above all, partners need to share our values of Privacy, Choice, and Sustainability.     

The list above is non-exhaustive. As we're a small team we have to
				prioritize certification of devices strategically. Our strategic
				objectives with this program are currently to prioritize device variety,
				easy DIY installation, regional availability and partners who are a
				positive force for good, both inside and outside of our community. We
				cannot always certify manufacturers who apply to join the program, but
				we will continue to work to increase our capacity over the coming
				months.
