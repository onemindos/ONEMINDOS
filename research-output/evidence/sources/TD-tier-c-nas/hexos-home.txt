HexOS is a force multiplier for TrueNAS, not a replacement. Install HexOS on an existing TrueNAS system you own, and unlock all the benefits and ease-of-use of HexOS while retaining the rock-solid foundation that TrueNAS provides.
Supercharge your TrueNAS system 
A dependable core
The full TrueNAS UI stays put. You always have full access to the underlying controls when necessary.
NFS, iSCSI, S3, directory services. You retain every protocol and service you run today.
Your pools, settings, users, shares, and apps, all stay put just the way you have them. HexOS meets your system where it is.
