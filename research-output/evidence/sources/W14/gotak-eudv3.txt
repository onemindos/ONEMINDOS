    Security architecture
    Your threat modelis your baseline.
Consumer phones defend against opportunistic malware. The EUD V3 defends against targeted exploitation, post-compromise persistence, and physical coercion. Security isn't a feature here — it's the architecture.

            The threat
            Your phone is exposing you.
            Zero-click exploits. Silent network signaling. Baseband-level attacks. Every app you install widens the attack surface.
This is not theoretical.
            The EUD V3 response
            Strict application sandboxing.
            GrapheneOS enforces strict application sandboxing — each app operates in its own isolated environment, restricted from interacting with other apps or accessing sensitive system resources without explicit user consent.
All apps run in isolated sandbox environments.
Compromised apps cannot access external resources or memory.
Google Play Services run inside the sandbox — no elevated privileges.
Most phones let one compromised app become a system-wide breach. This one doesn't.
            The threat
            The cellular modem is a black box.
            Cellular modems run outside the OS on closed-source firmware, in their own execution environment, with persistent network exposure whenever they're on. On most phones the modem is inherently trusted with direct memory access.
Unavoidable risk — as documented in Google's Project Zero CVE reports.
            The EUD V3 response
            The modem is treated as untrusted hardware.
            The EUD V3 hardware-isolates the cellular modem from the application processor, so a compromised baseband has no path into OS or app memory.
Hardware-isolates the modem from the application processor.
Prevents modem access to OS and app memory.
Zero-click baseband attacks can't escalate into full device compromise.
A modem exploit stays a modem exploit.
            The threat
            The classic five-dollar wrench attack.
            Security often fails under physical pressure. Users can be coerced to unlock devices. Biometric access can be forced. Standard locks offer no safe exit.
Border crossings, detentions, and checkpoints are security events.
            The EUD V3 response
            A safe exit under coercion.
            GrapheneOS is designed for real-world hostile scenarios — digital and physical. A pre-configured duress PIN triggers an irreversible process that destroys all hardware keystore keys, disk encryption keys, and eSIMs, then shuts the device down.
Duress PIN entry immediately wipes all user data under coercion.
Renders every encrypted byte permanently inaccessible.
Nothing left to extract, nothing left to negotiate.
Breaches happen under threat and pressure, not just malware.
            The threat
            Forensic extraction after unlock.
            Once unlocked, most phones become dramatically easier to extract from. Commercial forensic tools read active memory, capture running services, and exploit the expanded post-unlock window.
Time after unlock favors the attacker.
            The EUD V3 response
            Back to locked, automatically.
            The EUD V3 shrinks After-First-Unlock exposure — the window where decryption keys live in memory. Automatic reboot intervals return the device to the locked Before-First-Unlock state, the safest a device can be.
Automatic reboot intervals return the device to a locked state.
Rapid return to Before-First-Unlock — the safest device state.
Fewer persistence opportunities for forensic tools.
Smaller attack windows, less recoverable data.
            The threat
            Your apps are reporting back.
            Default smartphone permissions grant apps broad access to microphones, cameras, GPS, and the network. Most users never audit them. Apps routinely collect and transmit far beyond their stated function.
Data exfiltration usually happens in plain sight.
            The EUD V3 response
            You decide what each app can see, hear, and transmit.
            GrapheneOS provides per-app control over network connectivity, file access, and every device sensor. Cut an app off from the internet entirely, or deny it the microphone and accelerometer, with one toggle.
Per-app network toggles — block any app from reaching the internet.
Sensor controls for microphone, camera, accelerometer, and GPS.
Prevents unauthorized collection or transmission.
Nothing leaves the device unless you allow it.

    FAQ
    The questionsoperators ask first.
Full documentation in the GoTAK knowledge base. Community support on Discord. Procurement support direct from sales.
        Can I use the EUD V3 as my everyday phone?+
        Yes. It's a full smartphone — calls, messages, and any app you want to install — plus the complete TAK OS platform: CORTEX, Overwatch, RIDAR drone detection, SOOTHSAYER RF modeling, AOIs, and WARDEN device management. GrapheneOS runs Android apps natively, including a sandboxed, zero-privilege compatibility layer for apps that expect Google Play Services.
        What is CORTEX, and how is it different from a generic AI chatbot?+
        CORTEX is the cognitive layer of TAK OS — an agentic tactical advisor that runs on your EUD V3 with first-class TAK awareness. It can call tools (live KML overlays, threat graphs, WARDEN device status), bridge ATAK GeoChat conversations into its context, render inline maps, video, PDFs, and CoT bundles, and compose live dashboards on demand. It runs on pre-paid tokens, so there are no API keys to manage.
        Can I model RF coverage from the device?+
        Yes. SOOTHSAYER ships pre-loaded and is integrated into the Overwatch map. Drop a transmitter, pick a radio template — Silvus, TrellisWare, Harris AN/PRC, goTenna, Beartooth, or a custom build from a datasheet — and get a real-terrain coverage heatmap, a point-to-point link margin in dB, or a multisite composite, directly on the tactical map.
        Which carriers does it work with?+
        All of them. Every EUD V3 is carrier unlocked — Verizon, T-Mobile, AT&T, Mint, Google Fi, Vodafone, Orange, Movistar, or any other carrier worldwide. Dual SIM slots let you run two carriers at once, and our eSIM solution activates service in minutes. The only carriers we recommend avoiding are MVNOs that refuse out-of-network devices, such as Total Wireless.
        Can I use it outside the US?+
        Yes. Carrier unlocked means globally unlocked, and we ship to most countries with insured global transport. There are no import restrictions or usage regulations attached to the device.
        Does it work with my mesh network?+
        Yes. Out of the box the EUD V3 supports traditional mesh networks from Silvus, MPU5 and others, plus Beartooth MKII, goTenna Pro X2, and Meshtastic-based off-grid solutions. Grid down is no longer a factor for live mapping, tracking, voice, or UAS telemetry.
        Can I manage a fleet of EUD V3 devices remotely?+
        Yes. WARDEN MDM is built into TAK OS. Enroll devices from a QR code in device-owner mode, push policies that control the launcher, kiosk apps, network restrictions, and security settings, and issue lock, wipe, ring, or reboot commands that land in roughly three seconds. A full audit log is included.
        What happens if the device is taken?+
        You have a safe exit. Entering the pre-configured duress PIN irreversibly destroys all hardware keystore keys, disk encryption keys, and eSIMs, then powers the device down — leaving nothing to extract. Between unlocks, automatic reboots return the device to its most protected state, shrinking the window commercial forensic tools depend on.
        How long does shipping take?+
        Standard orders ship within 3–5 business days via UPS with 2–3 day delivery. Larger field kits are built to order and can take 7–30 days depending on configuration and stock. Expedited military procurement is available — contact sales.
        Is the EUD V3 NDAA compliant?+
        Yes. GoTAK devices are NDAA Section 889 compliant with no chips, modems, or components from banned vendors. TAA-compliant configurations and compliance documentation are available on request — email <email>
        What is the return policy?+
        Because every device is custom-provisioned and built to order, all sales are final — we don't offer refunds or returns. Choose your configuration carefully, and if you're unsure which setup fits your mission, talk to sales before ordering. The full refund policy is linked at the bottom of every page.
