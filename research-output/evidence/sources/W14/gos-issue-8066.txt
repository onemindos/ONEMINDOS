TITLE: Multicast issue when using Zero Tier

GrapheneOS's VPN leak protections are blocking multicast traffic over the Zero Tier VPN tunnel.

We have conducted some testing with both stock firmware and GrapheneOS.

For the test, we used the following link:
<shared-drive-link>

Using a Google Pixel 9a with the stock firmware, everything works as expected. Multicast traffic is functioning correctly, and the devices can see each other in ATAK with almost no configuration required.

However, when using a Google Pixel 9a running GrapheneOS, something appears to be preventing it from working properly. The setup is otherwise identical in every way.

I have been in contact with "other8026", who mentioned that the devs are currently working on the leakage protection.
