  Uncompromising
privacy
  With Volla OS, you decide for yourself what data you share with whom and when. It is based on the open source Android without Google Apps, Google Play Services and Google Cloud. There is also no Volla account. Your data belongs to you.
You can still use app that requires Google Play services with the open-source alternative microG.
Picture Text
  Protect your smartphone from surveillance, data thieves and malware with the unique security mode
Block specific apps that you don't trust but sometimes need to use.
Block specific Internet addresses of trackers, phishing, malware and more.
Block specific Internet connections from apps.
  Security Mode is perfect for protecting children or for ensuring that smartphones are used in a privacy-compliant manner in small businesses.

Longevity
  For the sake of the environment. Volla stands for longevity and long-term value.
High-quality hardware components and manufacturing
Long-term system updates of at least 5 years.
By cleaning the operating system of Google apps and services, data traffic to data centers is reduced by up to 80%, which are operated with a high energy consumption of small towns.
  Volla Cloud.Part of Volla OS
Common cloud services are processing and storing data in data centers with a central point of control. Operators can gain access to your data or be forced to provide access for 3rd parties.
Volla Cloud is created by networked smartphones and other devices without a single data center for storing and processing data. No third party even not Volla can access your data.
This gives the Volla Cloud a unique independence and security. It forms the digital ecosystem of the future.
Picture Text
  Volla Cloud Apps
One of the first applications is the secure Volla Messages app, which allows both highly encrypted exchange of messages to contacts with secure identity and moderated groups.
For each message exchange, whether to a person or a group, a separate network is created for maximum cyber security.
The Volla Cloud is based on the post-blockchain technology of Holochain, which uses the best of the blockchain concept while overcoming its disadvantages and limitations.

  Ubuntu Touch
  Freedom also means freedom of choice. Get a Volla phone or tablet with the mobile version of the popular Ubuntu Linux.
Picture Text
  If you already use Ubuntu Linux, you will feel right at home.
The handy sidebar allows you to quickly and easily access or switch between apps in any situation.
The fan view allows you to scroll through open apps.
As a Linux expert, you have full control over your device including root rights via the terminal app, the command line interface.
Ubuntu Touch adapts perfectly to the size of the display and transforms the Volla tablet with keyboard into a laptop for unrestricted productivity.
Convergence is the name of the concept that the future belongs to and Ubuntu Touch has already implemented.

  Two operating systems or more on one device
With the unique multi-boot function, you can install Volla OS and Ubuntu Touch on the same device. Then simply decide which operating system you want to use when starting up.
Other operating systems are also available for some models or are being prepared by the Volla developer community.
