 Why choose a support contract? Kairos is an open source tool — that means by default you only get best effort support from the community for bugs and issues. If you get stuck, it’s just you and the documentation. That’s great for when you’re evaluating a new technology, but if you’re relying on Kairos for production workloads, you need to know you can call on the experts for help when you need it most. You’ve got better things to do than dig around in Github for answers. That’s where Spectro Cloud’s Kairos support services come in.
