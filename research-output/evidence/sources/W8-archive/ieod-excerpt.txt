Introduction
                     Welcome to the documentation of the Industrial Edge Own Device (IEOD).
                     This is an image based on the Linux Debian 12 (bookworm) distribution and the Industrial
                     Edge Device Kit Version 1.24.1, that has been minimized and shaped for installing on a
                     compatible x86-64 hardware and converting that to an Edge Device.


Security information
                        – IEM must be reachable from assigned network, see Support Industry Siemens (https://
                          support.industry.siemens.com/cs/de/en/view/109782481).
                     • Minimum System Requirements of hardware:
                        – x86-64 architecture with UEFI boot support
                        – 1 CPU core
                        – 2 GB RAM
                        – 15 GB disk space on non-removable drive
                        – 1 Network Interface Card (NIC)


Does the IEOD support arm CPU architecture (e.g. Raspberry Pi)?
                     No, currently the IEOD does not support arm CPU architectures.
