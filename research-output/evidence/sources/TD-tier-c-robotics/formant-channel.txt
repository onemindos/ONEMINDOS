# Create a channel

A channel allows you to view a set of data streams, events, and notifications across devices. Just as we saw in [Create a view and add modules](https://docs.formant.io/docs/getting-started-create-a-view-and-add-modules), you can customize this interface to visualize data streams as you see fit.

# Step 1: Create a channel

1. In Formant, in the upper-left corner, open the menu.
2. Hover over the **Channels** section, and click the **plus icon** that appears to create a new channel.

<Image alt="Creating a new channel" align="center" src="https://files.readme.io/8fd0939-Screenshot_2023-10-27_at_3.05.12_PM.png">
  Creating a new channel.
</Image>

3. Give your channel a name and click **Continue**.
4. Select the data streams you want to monitor in this channel. You can either select the data streams manually, or choose a tag which relates to the relevant data streams.
5. Edit the layout of your channel view. You can add data modules as seen in [Create a view and add modules](https://docs.formant.io/docs/getting-started-create-a-view-and-add-modules).
6. Click **Save** to create your channel.

# Step 2: Verify your work

You'll now see your new channel in the **Channels** section of the Formant menu. You will see notifications for all data streams configured for this channel. By clicking on the channel, you can quickly access the view you created.

# Deleting a channel

To delete a channel, enter into the channel, click the three dots in the top right, and then click "Edit channel". Now, once in the edit mode, you will see a trash can icon. Click this to delete a channel.
