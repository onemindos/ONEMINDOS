Our mission at Plaid is to unlock financial freedom for everyone. Privacy and security are very important to us.

This Policy applies to Plaid Inc., Plaid Financial Ltd., and Plaid, B.V. (collectively, “Plaid,” “we,” “our,” and “us”). Please see the“Contacting Plaid” section below for which of these entities is responsible for processing your data.
