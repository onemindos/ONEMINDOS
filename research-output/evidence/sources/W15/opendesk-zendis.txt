From idea to innovation: openDesk’s story 
 openDesk grew out of the 'Sovereign Workplace' initiative launched by the German Federal Ministry of the Interior. 
 The Centre for Digital Sovereignty (ZenDiS GmbH), a publicly-funded limited liability company of the German Federal Government, took over openDesk’s development in January 2024. 
 Today, it works closely with German public authorities, established open source companies, and European partners.
