ABSTRACT: Create age-appropriate experiences in your app by asking people to share their age range.
BODY: Use the Declared Age Range API to request that people share their age range with your app. For children in a Family Sharing group, a parent or guardian or the Family Organizer can decide whether to always share a child’s age information with your app, ask the child every time, or never share their age information.
