FAQs
What's the more detailed difference between the ZimaOS and ZimaOS+ plan?
Choose ZimaOS
if you’re an individual, hobbyist, or casual user who needs core NAS functions, basic app deployments, and simple multi-member support.
Choose ZimaOS+
if you’re running a family setup, small team, or creative studio where
unlimited storage, and multiple members
are essential.
Is the $29 Plus license truly “lifetime”? Will it ever become a subscription?
ZimaOS Plus will absolutely never become a subscription.
We believe subscription models
conflict with
the core value proposition of private cloud, and therefore we will never adopt them.
How do I get Plus benefits on official Zima devices?
Automatic and lifetime.
Benefits are bound to your official device and will auto-activate, even after a system reinstall.
Is a ZimaOS+ License Tied to a Single Device or Transferable?
Each ZimaOS+ license is
bound to a single device.
This means your ZimaOS+ privileges
cannot be activated on multiple devices
at the same time under one license.
However, if you upgrade your hardware or need to replace your existing device, we provide a
license transfer option.
After your license has been active for at least one year, you can go to the
Order Management page
to
unbind
it from the old device and then
re-activate
it on a new one.
This
ensures flexibility
for users who upgrade their hardware while keeping the licensing system fair and secure.
What Hardware Drivers Does ZimaOS Support?
ZimaOS is designed to work seamlessly across a wide range of hardware environments. Out of the box, it includes drivers for:
· Mainstream x86 platforms
(Intel and AMD)
· Most SATA, NVMe, and USB storage controllers
· Popular network interface cards (NICs)
commonly used in PCs and servers
· GPU support (NVIDIA/AMD/Intel)
for hardware transcoding and AI workloads (varies by model and driver availability)
In general, if your hardware is supported by modern Linux kernels, it is very likely to be supported by ZimaOS as well. If you have additional driver requirements or encounter compatibility issues, we encourage you to reach out to us directly through the
ZimaOS Discord community.
Our team actively follows user feedback and continuously expands driver support based on community needs.
Does ZimaOS collect any telemetry or user data?
No.
From
day one, ZimaOS
has promised zero telemetry and
zero user data collection.
Everything you do
stays on your device.
This commitment is
openly stated in our privacy policy
and
reinforced
by ongoing
community oversight.
Do I need a cloud account to use ZimaOS?
No. ZimaOS
works entirely with local accounts.
You
don’t
need to sign up with an email or phone number, and you can run the system
completely offline.
This ensures your personal data and account information are
NEVER
tied to any external service.
When you purchase
a ZimaOS+ license, you will be asked to register an account.
This account
is used
ONLY
for managing your license purchase, order history, and activation. It has
NO
connection whatsoever to your ZimaOS usage, system configuration, or personal data.
How does ZimaOS handle remote access securely?
ZimaOS provides
high-performance remote access
using encrypted
peer-to-peer (P2P) connections. Unlike
traditional solutions that expose public ports or
rely on cloud relays,
ZimaOS minimizes attack surfaces and keeps your data safe during transmission. No ports are left open to the internet by default.
If I install ZimaOS on a virtual machine, can the Plus benefits be transferred to a real NAS device?
Yes.
In special cases, we will provide manual reissuance or support.
