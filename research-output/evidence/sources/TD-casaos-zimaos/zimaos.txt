Designed for privacy and safety
Stand against tracking and analyzing any user data_
Local Account System
ZimaOS uses a self-generated device identity to
keep your account fully offline
. No emails, phone numbers or platform logins are required. Your data and identity remain yours alone, beyond the reach of any third party.
Storage Redundancy
Storage redundancy prepares your system for the unexpected. From mirrored drives with
RAID 1
to fault-tolerant setups with
RAID 5 or 6
, every layer is designed to protect against loss. Even older drives can be reused through JBOD for long-term archiving.
Peer to Peer Networking
P2P connection keeps your data between devices
without touching any server or cloud
. Everything is encrypted and travels directly from source to destination, so even when accessing your NAS from a café or hotel Wi-Fi, nothing leaves your private network.
Multiple Users
For families or small teams sharing one device, ZimaOS allows the main account to set folder-level access for each member. Personal folders stay private by default, with only shared spaces visible to others.

Co-creation is part of our DNA
Building ZimaOS with our community_
From CasaOS to ZimaOS, 43,000+ community members have driven Zima's evolution. Our vibrant hub fosters shared experiences, ideas, and learning, shaping Zima's future with us.
DLNA
Inspired by Escaperoute, Devtechk, Protektor
DLNA lets you wirelessly stream local photos, music, and videos to DLNA-compatible devices like TVs and speakers—no copying or complex setup needed, just plug and play.
ZVM
Inspired by cfouche, Robert
ZVM is a lightweight virtual machine system built into ZimaOS. It supports one-click installation of Windows and popular Linux distributions, as well as custom image setups—making it easy to run multiple operating systems for development, testing, or isolated environments.
UPS
Inspired by Ghomzi, Neofight, The Vanishing_Dragon
ZimaOS supports UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) integration to safely shut down the system during power outages, protecting your device and data. Ideal for long-running and critical tasks.
Btop
Inspired by scyto, NekoYuki
Btop is a fast and interactive resource monitor that shows real-time usage of CPU, memory, disk, and network. It offers a clear and responsive interface, making it easy to track system performance.
