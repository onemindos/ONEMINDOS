Pricing FAQ
Do you have a per-app pricing model?
 We are not in the business of reselling apps.
 We make it simpler to manage self-hosted apps and as such our pricing model is to charge for that solution.
Do you have access to my server?
 Cloudron is entirely self-hosted and we do not have access to your server.
 Just like smartphones, app and platform updates are pulled, not pushed.
