 Looking for Ubuntu Pro on public clouds? 
 Ubuntu Pro on public clouds is metered by the hour and charged through your cloud provider’s bill. Pricing is
 relative to your compute cost, typically between 3–4.5% of the list price. For exact rates, please refer to the
 pricing pages for AWS , Azure and GCP .
 Please note, you can also deprecate your committed spend with Ubuntu Pro.
