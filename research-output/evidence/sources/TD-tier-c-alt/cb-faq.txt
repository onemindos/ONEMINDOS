🖥️ Hardware & Performance

💾
What storage does ClawBox use — eMMC or SSD?

ClawBox comes with a 512GB NVMe SSD for significantly better I/O performance when loading AI models and handling database operations. The speed difference is especially noticeable with larger language models.
⚡
How much power does it actually use?

ClawBox draws just 20W under normal AI workloads — about €0.80/month in electricity (at €0.25/kWh). Even under heavy inference, it stays below 25W. That's less than most light bulbs. Compare that to a Mac Mini at 65W or a gaming PC at 300W+.
🌡️
Does it get hot running 24/7?

The Jetson Orin Nano has excellent thermal design. ClawBox stays cool thanks to its optimized cooling system with a near-silent fan. The carbon color case provides excellent heat dissipation. No thermal throttling even during continuous 24/7 operation.
🧠
Can it run larger models?

On the device, ClawBox works best with small models such as the preinstalled Gemma 4 E2B (8 GB unified memory). For bigger tasks you can turn on ClawBox AI or connect another cloud provider. That is optional: local stays the default.
🤖
What AI models come pre-installed?

ClawBox ships with Gemma 4 E2B, which runs locally on the device with no account and no internet Whisper speech-to-text is an optional one-click install in the Local AI tab. ClawBox AI (cloud) is optional for heavier tasks, and you can add your own API keys or other local models anytime.

🔧 Setup & Software

📦
How complex is the initial setup?

Literally 5 minutes: (1) Unbox and connect power + Ethernet (or use built-in dual-band WiFi), (2) Navigate to clawbox.local in your browser, (3) Complete the configuration wizard, (4) Start chatting via Telegram. Bluetooth 5.0 lets you pair speakers, microphones, and other accessories. No Docker, CUDA drivers, or Linux expertise required.View Setup Guide →
🔐
Does any data leave my network?

By default, OpenClaw memory, files, automations, and supported local workflows stay on your ClawBox. Cloud API usage is opt-in; Claude runs in Anthropic's cloud and is ideal when you choose premium reasoning for a specific task.
🏠
Does it work with my existing smart home setup?

Yes! ClawBox integrates with Home Assistant, MQTT, REST APIs, and most popular smart home platforms. It can control lights, thermostats, security systems, and more through voice commands or automated rules.
