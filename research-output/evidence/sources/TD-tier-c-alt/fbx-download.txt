 See further instructions on how to build your own FreedomBox by downloading and setting up on your hardware of choice.

 The FreedomBox operating system is compatible with any hardware that Debian is compatible with, but we also target our system for specific models of single-board computers for ease of setup and consistent experience. Single-board computers are affordable, increasingly fast, and power-efficient, which makes them great hardware for FreedomBox. You can download Stable and Testing versions of FreedomBox for the hardware models listed below.
