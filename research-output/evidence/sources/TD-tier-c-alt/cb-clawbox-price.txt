Weekly allowances are rolling 7-day limits
They are not calendar weeks, and nothing resets on a set day. Each allowance counts what you used over the last 7 days, and as each day drops out of that window, what you used on it becomes available again. Chat also has a burst limit of 25% of the weekly chat allowance in any 5 hours, so one runaway task cannot use up the whole week.
