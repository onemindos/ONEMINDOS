What is Hermes Agent, and who are Nous Research?

Short version, because the name is overloaded and two different things share it.

Hermes Agent is an open-source autonomous AI agent from Nous Research, an AI research lab. It is MIT-licensed and developed in the open on GitHub. Unlike a chat window, it runs persistently, keeps memory across sessions, and builds reusable skills from what it has already done. Its OpenRouter listing describes 40+ built-in tools, including web search and browser automation.

Because it is designed to run continuously, it needs somewhere to live. Nous's own documentation lists seven terminal backends — local, Docker, SSH, Singularity, Modal, Daytona and Vercel Sandbox — so the software is deliberately agnostic about the machine. That machine is the part we build.

Hermes Agent is not the same thing as Hermes 3. Nous Research also publishes the Hermes family of open-weight language models, and Hermes 3 8B is one of them. Hermes Agent is the agent that does the work; a Hermes model is one of many models it can be pointed at. ClawBox Hermes Edition ships the agent, and you choose the model — locally through Ollama, or from a cloud provider you bring.

Questions people ask before buying

 How fast does it ship? 

We build to order rather than stockpile, so 1–3 working days is our commitment to ship, not to deliver. When your order comes in we flash your unit, run the full test suite on it, and hand it to DHL. Transit time then depends on where you are — typically 1–3 days inside the EU.
 What does it cost to run? 

Electricity is about €39 a year at 7–15 W. Beyond that it depends on your AI provider: local models through Ollama cost nothing per token, and a cloud API is billed by that provider directly — typically €5–20 a month for personal use. We don't take a cut and we don't require a subscription.
 Can it run OpenClaw as well? 

Hermes Edition ships running Hermes Agent only, so out of the box it has one job and one attack surface. It's the same hardware as our OpenClaw Edition, and you can switch between the two on the device with a single click — so the edition you order is where you start, not where you are stuck.
 How private is it, really? 

The box sits on your network with no inbound ports open. Your provider keys are stored on the device, not with us. With a local model selected, nothing leaves your home at all. You get full root and SSH access — we can't see inside your box, and neither can anyone else without your say-so.
 What happens when Hermes releases a breaking change? 

You're not tracking upstream directly — we are. We pin a known-good release, test it on the same hardware you own, and only then push it to you. Security fixes are fast-tracked ahead of the normal release train.

ID Robots OOD, a robotics and edge-AI company in Plovdiv, Bulgaria. We've been shipping ClawBox hardware since 2026 — every unit is assembled, flashed and tested by us, in the EU.
