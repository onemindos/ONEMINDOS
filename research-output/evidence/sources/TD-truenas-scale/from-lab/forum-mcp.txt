 In what sense? The API and the MCP tools themselves are of course fully deterministic. Meaning you or an agent calls the tool with your parameters, that is mapped into the API and is deterministic the whole way through.
 Of course that has nothing to say about the LLM or the Human + LLM combination on the other end of the toolset. Both of those tend to be non deterministic, although I would say with the latest frontier models, they are trending towards being more reliably deterministic than the human counterpart sitting at the keyboard tends to be. Anybody who’s been interacting with a user community for any decent amount of time should know that

 MikeO3:
 SCALE auth rate-limits rapid tn-ws.py reconnects — space calls or keep a session.
 FYI this is misuse of websocket protocol. The point of having a stateful protocol is that you don’t rapidly create / tear down. You should keep sessions. It’s really no different than SMB in that way for example (unless you’re a PBS developer – after years of still doing SMB sessions wrong with resulting server load and log spam) .
