Frequently asked questions
Is Talos Linux still free?
Yes. Talos Linux community edition is free and open source under MPL-2.0, and stays that way.
Enterprise Linux is the same codebase with a commercial layer on top.
What is Talos Enterprise Linux, and what does it cost?
The commercial tier of Talos Linux: $1,000 per node per year, with a 10-node minimum and flat per-node pricing. It adds FIPS 140-3 compliant builds, SBOMs with curated VEX, signed attestations, CVE SLAs, commercial IP indemnity, 24/7 support, and Omni fleet management, included.
Is Omni bundled or sold separately?
Both. The full Omni management plane is included with Talos Enterprise Linux. Omni is also available standalone on Talos Linux community edition through the Hobby and Startup plans, without the enterprise assurance layer.
What's the difference between standalone Omni and the Omni in Enterprise Linux?
Standalone Omni (Hobby, Startup) manages clusters on the free community OS. Enterprise Linux adds FIPS-compliant builds, CVE SLAs, indemnity, signed attestations, and 24/7 support on top.
Do I need Kubernetes to run virtual machines?
No. Talos Hypervisor runs VMs directly on Talos Linux without Kubernetes. Open source with general availability in December.
Can I self-host or run in an air-gapped environment?
Yes. Enterprise Linux and Omni run as managed SaaS or fully self-hosted, including disconnected and air-gapped environments.
