## 1. How to read this file

**Which answer wins when sources disagree.** (1) For the 23 questions that are the *primary* bank question of a decision-critical item (DC-01…DC-24; DC-02 has no bank question), the **latest Result block (rev. 4 where one exists, otherwise rev. 3)** in `evidence/decision-critical.md` sets the status and opens the answer, and the workstream detail follows it. (2) For the 30 questions no workstream answered, and for the two cases where owners disagreed (Q300; Q284 follows its owner W11), the answer was written here (marked *QBANK*). (3) Otherwise the **owning workstream's** row wins over rows that only give input. Other files that also answered a question are listed under "Also in".

**Revision drift.** Several workstream rows were written on 2026-11-10, before `01` was revised twice. Where a row still says "ADR-001 chose Rugix" or similar, `01` rev. 4 wins: **RAUC** (built from a pinned upstream tag, today v1.15.2) is the update engine on x86 and Pi 5, Mender is the named runner-up, and Rugix is the documented alternative. **Base OS (rev. 4):** the rev-3 three-way tie-break that kept Debian is withdrawn. Under the frozen rules the S1 decision of record is **Ubuntu 26.04 LTS + RAUC** (Ubuntu 65 · bootc 64 · Debian 63; 6–4 over bootc; fragile, 11 of 14 runs). It is conditional on Z-01 and G5, and a Debian 13 leg is built from the same definition until the base is locked (`01` §3.1.6, §7.3). **Rows that say "on Debian" describe the Debian leg**, which is the pre-registered result if W2-HW-3 fails. Only rows whose answer changes under rev. 4 were rewritten (Q2–Q4, Q8, Q125, Q128, Q129, Q288, Q324–Q326, Q335 and §3, §6).

**Status rules.** The status column uses exactly the six values of the brief. A `partial` keeps its outstanding blocker in the **Remaining** column instead of being counted twice. "Counsel" questions are counted as `founder-decision`, because the brief says to pick, state the assumption and leave the call to Zeus (rule 8).

**Columns in §4:** ID · priority (P0 blocks the foundation, P1 the 1–2 year plan, P2 the 5–10 year horizon) · the question (shortened; full text in `context/05`) · status · remaining blocker · answer · claim IDs · where the full answer lives · DC link · other files that also answered it.
