The lock screen follows the Atlas design (design-v2 screen 11): the Contour wallpaper softly blurred, the box's name top left, a big clock and date, a card that welcomes the person back by name with their initials, the number of decisions waiting, and a line that says where the sign-in goes (no cloud account; encrypted connection, the box's own screen, or a warning on plain http). Unlocking offers every D-034 way in, and sign-in now shows the password and authenticator-code form beside the passkey button instead of hiding it under "Other ways", because passkeys are optional. New visual screen dash-lock (light and dark).
