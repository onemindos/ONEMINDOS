Files has a Storage limits tab for the owner: what each person's files use and the limit they may reach, with a field to change one person's limit or remove it. `onemind files quotas list` and `quotas set PERSON --gb N or --none` do the same, and `onemind files stat PATH` shows the details of one file or folder.
