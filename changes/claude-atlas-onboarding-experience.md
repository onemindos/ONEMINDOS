First run, part A of the mixed experience the founder picked (the map draws itself, plus the You decide and Your place pages): the first step now names the place as well as the box, and the setup map starts empty and draws itself once the place is named. A new address step lets the owner type their address and move the map there, offline first: coordinates or a link copied from a map app are read on the device, a street address alone is not looked up because the box has no address index, and the screen says so; the owner can also ask the device for its position through the browser's own prompt, which says the browser may ask its maker's location service. The place (name, the owner's address text, three grid points around the position) is saved through POST /v1/sites as the box's first site, which gains an optional address field. A You decide step practises the approval rule with a simulated request (hold to approve, or tap then confirm, or say no; optional, calls no API). Each answer adds one thing to the map (a ring for the approval rule, a tile per waiting install request, a dashed line for a backup choice) and setup ends on the desktop Map when this build has it. Reduced motion, Escape and focus rules stay as before.
