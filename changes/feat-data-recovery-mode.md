Recovery mode when the data disk fails (gap found in the umbrelOS image study). Before, a missing or broken `onemind-data` partition failed `local-fs.target` and stopped the box in a systemd emergency shell with no network. Now `/data` and its bind mounts are `nofail` (30 s device timeout), `onemind-data-check.service` decides before `local-fs.target`, and on failure the box isolates `onemind-recovery.target`: network and mDNS stay up, temporary state lives in RAM, a read-only status page answers 503 on `onemind.local:8080` (`/status.json` too), and the alert goes to the console, the login banner and the journal (fixed MESSAGE_ID). It never formats or repairs the disk and runs no k3s, apps or AI. A failed disk is not held against an update: RAUC's pre-install handler also writes its trial marker to the ESP, recovery mode resets the try counter only of an already-committed slot, and a trial that runs out while its boots were in recovery gets the gate verdict `environment-bad` (re-offered) instead of `image-bad`. Design and six founder calls in `os/recovery/README.md`; QEMU battery TD1-TD3 pre-registered in `test/qemu/CRITERIA-DATAFAIL.md`.
