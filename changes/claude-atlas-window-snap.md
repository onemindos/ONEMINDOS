Desktop windows get the Atlas window chrome and snap layouts (final design screens 01 and 01b). Every window now has a 42 px title bar with the screen's icon and name and its buttons on the right (minimise, workspace, maximise, close); assistive tech still hears the window's name once. Hovering the maximise button, or pressing the down arrow on it, opens a snap layouts flyout with four layouts (halves, wide left, wide right, thirds); each part puts the window there, also out of maximised, and Esc closes only the flyout. Alt Shift Left and Alt Shift Right snap the focused window to the left or right half; both are in the keyboard shortcuts sheet. Tiling and phones place windows themselves, so they show no flyout.
