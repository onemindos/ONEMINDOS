Recovery contacts, the first step of "never lose your data" (gateway permissions design 25d, D-028). An owner invites up to 5 people who can later confirm that a locked-out owner is really them: an adult of the household (from their own session) or someone outside it through the OneMind phone app, with no account on the box. The box signs each invite with its own key (single use, 7 days), so the phone pins the box key; accepting registers the phone's P-256 contact key with a proof of possession and returns, once, a contact token and the 32-byte contact secret for the offline code (kept on the box sealed with AES-GCM). A new contact counts only after a cooling-off the owner picks (3, 7 or 14 days, 7 by default). Adding a contact or shortening the wait needs an owner session and the passkey step-up (L1 today, L2 once verification levels land); removing one is L0. The contact principal reaches only its own three routes (status, notices, resign) and gets 403 everywhere else, and every owner and contact is told of each change. API under /v1/auth/recovery and /v1/recovery, `onemind recovery contacts`, `settings` and `invitations`, and a Recovery contacts card in Settings, Account. Contact-assisted recovery itself comes in 25e.
