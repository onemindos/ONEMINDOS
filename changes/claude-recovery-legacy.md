Legacy contacts and the claim process (gateway permissions design 25f, D-028). An owner names up to 5 people who may get a read-only copy of the owner's files after the owner's death, added like recovery contacts (owner session and passkey step-up, box-signed invite, cooling-off). On accepting, the contact's phone gets a legacy access key once; the box keeps only a salted verifier. A claim needs that key and a death certificate (PDF, JPEG or PNG, which OneMind cannot verify; it is kept for the household's owners and adults as evidence). Then a wait the owner picked (14, 30, 60 or 90 days, 30 by default) with notices to every owner and adult and every other contact at the start, every 7 days and on the last day. Any sign-in by the owner cancels the claim at once, and it completes only if the owner did not sign in, use a key or approve anything for the whole wait. When another legacy contact or a household adult exists, one of them must confirm as the second person. The contact then gets a read-only export of the owner's Files home (a file list and a tar download) for 12 months; nothing is ever deleted, and the legacy principal reaches only its own routes, so it never gets admin, other people's data, app secrets or keys. Owners cancel a claim (and may block the contact) at L0. API under /v1/auth/legacy and /v1/legacy, `onemind legacy contacts` and `onemind legacy claims`, and Legacy cards in Settings, Account.
