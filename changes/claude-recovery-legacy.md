Legacy contacts and the claim process (gateway permissions design 25f, D-028). An owner names up to 5 people who may get a read-only copy of the owner's files after the owner's death, added like recovery contacts (owner session and passkey step-up, box-signed invite, cooling-off). On accepting, the contact's phone gets a legacy access key once; the box keeps only a salted verifier. A claim needs that key and a death certificate (PDF, JPEG or PNG, which OneMind cannot verify; it is kept for the household's owners and adults as evidence). Then a wait the owner picked (14, 30, 60 or 90 days, 30 by default) with notices to every owner and adult and every other contact at the start, every 7 days and on the last day. Any sign-in by the owner cancels the claim at once, and it completes only if the owner did not sign in, use a key, use one of their own personal access tokens or approve anything for the whole wait (a script or app that calls the box every day with the owner's personal token therefore keeps a claim from completing; automated agent, plugin, node and automation tokens do not count. Whether automated use should count is a founder decision for Zeus). When another legacy contact or a household adult exists, one of them must confirm as the second person. The contact then gets a read-only export of the owner's Files home (a file list and a tar download) for 12 months; nothing is ever deleted, and the legacy principal reaches only its own routes, so it never gets admin, other people's data, app secrets or keys. Owners cancel a claim (and may block the contact) at L0. API under /v1/auth/legacy and /v1/legacy, `onemind legacy contacts` and `onemind legacy claims`, and Legacy cards in Settings, Account. Every claim write is a compare-and-set on the state it expects, so an owner's sign-in that cancels a claim can never be overwritten back to granted. The household's claim list shows who confirmed next to the claimant and flags a claimant who accepted the invite on a signed-out phone. The export is streamed (nothing is staged in a temporary file), one download per contact at a time. Whether a second person exists is checked again when the wait ends, and the claimant's own account never counts as one. At most one open claim per owner, enforced by the database. The owner can also end a granted export from Settings, Account.

Sign-in, owner activity and cancellation also stop a download already in progress. The download slot is shared by gateway workers on the box and is released when the request ends, including on disconnect.
