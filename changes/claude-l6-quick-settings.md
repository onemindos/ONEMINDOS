A Quick settings button in the menu bar, just before the bell (#841). It opens a small panel with the everyday switches: light, dark or automatic theme through the appearance provider, a Simple or Pro choice that follows the person's role (an owner or adult may narrow it to Simple for themselves, a guest, child or teen cannot pick Pro), quiet hours on or off through the notification preferences with If-Match and a re-read on a conflict, and Stop all agents for the owner only after a confirm. Below them four read-only tiles show network, updates waiting, last backup and free storage with how fresh each answer is; free storage uses the measured filesystem bytes from GET /v1/storage, and a route that fails or a value the box could not measure reads Unknown, never a green state. The SDK gains notifications.preferences.get and save so the dashboard can send If-Match.
