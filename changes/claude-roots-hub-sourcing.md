The hub now keeps a copy of every joined node's leaf outbox across the per-node NATS accounts (#747). Each NODE account exports to the hub only its outbox consumer API, the flow-control replies and a delivery subject under its own prefix, and the hub imports them; approving a join makes the node's hub source stream (the shape of internal/leaf HubSourceStream). Each node's copy keeps the registry retention of node events (7 days, 256 MiB, less on a small hub disk, oldest dropped first), so one chatty or compromised node fills only its own copy. A two-leaf lab test proves a node cut for 10 minutes while writing 600 events delivers exactly those 600 in order, and that node A reaches neither node B's stream nor its JetStream API; a second one kills the link silently with events in flight and proves none is lost, because nats-server acknowledges the WorkQueue outbox only for what the hub stored. Known gap with a follow-up: the empty flow-control replies still reach every node until leaves turn on the js_ack_fc_v2 flag.
