The desktop, phone and desktop app take the colours, wallpapers and theme packs of the founder's final design. Backgrounds are Sovereign stone (light #e8e7e3, dark #0d0e10), every button is signal red #cc1f2b with white text in both modes and in every pack (dark mode was #fb605a), the highlight is the design's teal, and the design's missing colours are tokens now (needs you, fault, plugin and network layers, simulated, focused window edge, and the six live states). Console's wallpaper set is complete: Ripple, Dawn, Harvest, Mycelium and Ember are added and Contour, Signal and Lattice are redrawn to match, all generated in code, with Contour the default. The earlier wallpapers stay selectable. The two Console packs are renamed OneMind and OneMind Light, and each of the ten packs opens on the wallpaper the design names. Windows get a 14 px radius (was 24 px) and the corner scale follows the design.
