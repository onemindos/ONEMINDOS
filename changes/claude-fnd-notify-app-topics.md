Per-app notification topics and app principals (#613). Each built-in now has an app principal (id app:ID, in-process only, never a token, cannot request or approve) and posts to the one inbox under the topics its descriptor declares, as app.ID.TOPIC, info or warning only, and only while it is switched on; a descriptor topic may now carry a title instead of catalogue events. Every inbox item and its notification.created stream event say where it came from: source_kind (system, node, app, plugin or agent), app_id (the app chip, also on system items such as a failed backup), and optionally asset_id (the map asset), group_key and up to three action buttons (open an in-app link, or file a normal request that still needs an approval; there is no approve button). POST /v1/notifications/send takes the same fields, and an installed app's agent shows its plugin chip. New GET /v1/notifications/sources lists every app and plugin source with its topics, mute state and a one-hour rate meter, and PUT /v1/notifications/sources/ID lets an owner mute or unmute one (agents and adults cannot); a source that posts more than 60 times in 10 minutes is muted by the gateway and the owners get one note. System alerts are never muted. Preference routes accept app.ID.* topics, the Notifications built-in lists the new Settings page and backs up the new notify_sources table, and an upgrade adds the new inbox columns.
