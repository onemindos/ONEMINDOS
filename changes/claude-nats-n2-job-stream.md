A joined machine now gets the jobs its gateway signs even when it was off or cut off at the time (D-029 plan N2, fixes "no program on the machine listens for jobs"). The hub keeps every job in the ONEMIND_JOBS work queue, one copy per job id, and the gateway gives each joined node its own durable pull consumer (node-<node_id>) when the join is approved. The node's NATS account may call only two hub services, "next" and "ack" of its own consumer, so a machine can read only its own jobs and still cannot publish one. onemindd's new job intake (internal/jobs) pulls one job at a time, checks it with the onemind.job/v2 verifier from #1165 (signed by the pinned key, addressed to this node, not expired, not older than the policy epoch, never seen), records the job id before it acknowledges and runs it, so a job runs at most once and a replay is refused, and reports the result on onemind.node.<id>.jobresult.<job_id>. OS rollout offers are published named by job id. Only the read-only host.check kind runs on the machine today; update.apply is reported unsupported_kind until the update service takes jobs (next step). Nothing on NATS approves anything.
