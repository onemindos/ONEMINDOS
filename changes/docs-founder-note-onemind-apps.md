Founder note 14 is a web page about OneMind's own apps: the ten registered built-ins, the fifteen apps that are built but not yet registered, and the seventeen planned, each with its issue. It compares built-in, Market and custom apps (D-026), shows how an AI agent reaches any app through the gateway's MCP door (read tools and request-only tools; only a person approves), and recommends what to build next. It adds no decision and lists the three founder decisions still open.

Founder note 15 explains how one OneMind app serves every module on every device: one shell per platform (web desktop, phone, headset), module screens sent as data and drawn by two renderers (#641, #642), and hand-built screens only for Approvals, Map, Photos and Files. It recommends owning the experience and borrowing open-source engines underneath, and a 3D split (MapLibre 2D, CesiumJS outdoor 3D, three.js indoor). The own-vs-borrow rules and the 3D split wait on the founder.
