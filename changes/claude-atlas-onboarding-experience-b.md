First run, part B of the mixed experience: the desktop assembles itself as the owner picks apps. From the apps step on, a small copy of the Atlas dock (Home, Map, Approvals, Files, Market) sits under the setup map, and each ticked app drops into it with a waiting badge, because a ticked app is only an install request until the owner approves it; unticking takes it out again. At most six tiles move at once, reduced motion turns it into a fade, and screen readers get one sentence describing the dock. Finishing setup now fades the steps out while the map grows a little toward the desktop Map window that opens next; if finishing fails the wizard comes back with the reason.
