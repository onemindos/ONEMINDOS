Contact-assisted recovery and the remote wait (gateway permissions design 25e, D-028). A locked-out owner starts a recovery from the sign-in page or `onemind recovery start` and gets a 2-digit matching number and a 15-minute request code. One of their recovery contacts then confirms in their app by picking the matching number out of three and signing with their contact key, or, with no network path, by reading back an 8-digit code (an HMAC of the request under the contact secret) that the owner types. Wrong answers close the request after 3 tries, and an owner can start at most one contact recovery a day, enforced in the database so parallel starts and parallel guesses cannot get past it (the try is taken before the answer is compared). The contact routes cannot start a recovery, but the start route is public: a single contact who turns hostile can start one and confirm alone with the 8-digit code, and the wait plus the notices to every owner and contact are the safeguard. Then comes a wait the owner chose (24, 48 or 72 hours, 24 by default), raised to 72 by itself when a strong key was used in the last 7 days. With no contact, a password plus the authenticator code (never a password alone) starts a remote recovery that waits 72 hours or 7 days. Every owner and every contact is told at the start, every 24 hours and in the last hour, and any owner session, any contact or the person who started it can cancel at once. After the wait the starter enrols a new passkey (and on the contact path may set a new password, which signs out the old sessions) and gets a normal session. Nothing is approved, deleted or unlocked by any of this. Running recoveries show on Settings, Account with a Stop button, and the waits are set there or with `onemind recovery settings`. A cancel and a finish that land at the same moment can no longer disagree: every state change, including starting a request and taking a try, is a compare-and-set or a database constraint, so whichever commits first wins, the other changes nothing, and only the winner's notice is sent.
