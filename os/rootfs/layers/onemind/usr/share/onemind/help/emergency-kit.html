Emergency kit

Your emergency kit holds the recovery key for your backups. You saved it once, when you set up backups, and typed two of its groups back to prove you had it.

What the key looks like

Thirteen groups of four characters (the letters A to Z and the digits 2 to 7), followed by one check group.

Why it matters

  • The box never stores this key. If the box is lost or broken, this key is how you get back into your backups.
  • The backup password is the thirteen groups joined with dashes, exactly as printed. With it you can restore your backups with the free tool restic, even without a OneMind box.

Keep it safe

  • Keep it on paper or in a password manager, away from the box.
  • If you lose it while the box still works, make a new kit and save it. A new kit adds a new key; the old key is not removed, so destroy any old copy you find.
