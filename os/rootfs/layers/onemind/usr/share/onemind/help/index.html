OneMind offline help

These pages live on your box. They open without the internet and without the OneMind desktop, so you can read them when something is wrong.

Something is wrong

  • The desktop does not open, or apps stopped: use the rescue console. It shows the system slots, downloads the logs and can roll the box back to the previous OS version.
  • The box is lost or broken and you need your backups: you need your emergency kit, the recovery key you saved when you set up backups.

Nothing in these pages changes your box. Every action in the rescue console asks you to confirm it first.
