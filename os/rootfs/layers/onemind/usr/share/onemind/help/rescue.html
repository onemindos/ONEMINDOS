Rescue console

The rescue console is a small page that runs on the box itself, outside the apps and the OneMind desktop. It keeps working when those are down.

Open it

  1. Use a computer or phone on the same home network as the box. The console refuses visitors from the internet and from your other OneMind places.
  2. Open https:// followed by the box's network address and :7443. Your router's list of connected devices shows the box's address.
  3. When the browser asks you to sign in, type any user name. The password is your owner token.

After five wrong passwords in one minute, that device has to wait a minute before it can try again.

What it can do

  • Slots. The box keeps two copies of the operating system, slot A and slot B. The console shows which one is running and the version in each.
  • Roll back. Starts the other slot, which holds the previous OS version, and restarts the box. Type rollback to confirm. Reconnect after a few minutes.
  • Logs. Downloads the recent system logs (OneMind, updates, the health check, RAUC and k3s) as a text file you can keep or send to someone who helps you.
  • Terminal. Shown only when the owner has switched it on at the box with onemindd rescue terminal enable. Opening a shell needs a fresh passkey sign-in.
